# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Critique claims and methods with
evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, and distinguish disagreement from personal
criticism. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misrepresentation of
research results, and disclosure of others' private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to
protect contributors. Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer
through a contact method they have actually published; do not post sensitive
details in a public issue. A project-specific reporting address has not yet been
established and must be added by the owner before public community operation.
